"Shelter Home for Women (SHW)" is a Social Welfare Scheme by the Directorate of Women & Child Development, Govt. of Goa. The
scheme provides temporary shelter and support to women who have no family or social support systems to rely on and rehabilitate the
women socially and economically by the provision of skill training, and counselling. The shelter Homes are given Financial Assistance
in the form of grants consisting of salary components and maintenance expenditures.BenefitsThe Shelter Homes are given Financial
Assistance in the form of Grants consisting of Salary Components and Maintenance Expenditures.EligibilityThe applicant should qualify
as ANY ONE of the following - A Non-Government Voluntary Organization registered under the Registration of Societies Act, 1860.A
Public Trust, registered under any law for the time being in force.Local Body.Government Body.Body as specified by the Govt. having a
Managing Body with powers, duties and responsibilities, the experience of working in the field of women and children welfare, and
rendering their services without distinction of caste, religion or language.Application ProcessOfflineStep 1: Visit the Directorate of
Women & Child Development, Panaji-Goa, and request a hard copy of the proforma of the application form for the scheme from the
Concerned Authority.Step 2: In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across),
and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest if required).Step 3: Submit the duly filled and signed application form along
with the documents to the Concerned Authority.Step 4: Acquire the receipt/acknowledgement of the successful submission of the
application.Documents RequiredProof of Registration.Details of the Admitted Women.Details of the Rehabilitation Measures (Skill
Training, and Counselling).Bank Details (for the purpose of transfer of grants).Frequently Asked QuestionsCan Women Of Any Age
Avail Of The Benefits Of The Shelter Home For Women Scheme?The guidelines doesn't specify an age limit for women availing the
benefits of the scheme. However, it's likely that the focus is on providing assistance to women who lack family or social support systems
and need temporary shelter and rehabilitation.What Type Of Financial Assistance Is Provided To Shelter Homes Under This Scheme?
Shelter Homes receive financial assistance in the form of grants, which includes salary components for staff and maintenance
expenditures.Which Organizations Are Eligible To Apply For The SHW Scheme?Non-Government Voluntary Organizations registered
under the Registration of Societies Act, 1860, public trusts registered under any applicable law, local bodies, government bodies, or other
specified bodies with experience in women and children welfare work and providing services without any discrimination are eligible to
apply.How Can Eligible Organizations Apply For The SHW Scheme?Eligible organizations need to follow a four-step process to apply
for the scheme. They should visit the Directorate of Women & Child Development, Panaji-Goa, request the application form, fill it with
required details, and submit it along with necessary documents to the Concerned Authority.What Are The Mandatory Documents
Required For Applying To The SHW Scheme?The mandatory documents include proof of registration of the organization, details of the
admitted women, details of rehabilitation measures (skill training and counseling), and bank details for the purpose of grant transfer.Is
There Any Reservation, Relaxation, Preference, Or Priority Category Specified For This Scheme?The content doesn't mention any
reservation, relaxation, preference, or priority category, indicating that all eligible organizations have equal chances of availing the
benefits of the scheme.What Is The Purpose Of The Directorate Of Women & Child Development's Citizen Charter Document
Mentioned In The Guidelines?The Directorate of Women & Child Development's Citizen Charter document provides detailed guidelines
and information about the SHW Scheme and the application process. It serves as a reference for organizations interested in applying for
the scheme.How Does The Skill Training Provided By The Scheme Contribute To The Rehabilitation Of Women In The Shelter Homes?
The skill training offered by the scheme helps women in shelter homes to acquire valuable skills that can empower them economically
and socially. These skills can lead to potential job opportunities and financial independence.Are There Any Specific Types Of
Counseling Services Provided Under The Scheme?The content doesn't specify the types of counseling services offered. However,
counseling generally helps the women cope with emotional and psychological challenges, supporting their overall well-being and
rehabilitation.Sources And ReferencesCitizen's CharterOkWas this helpful?News and UpdatesNo new news and updates
