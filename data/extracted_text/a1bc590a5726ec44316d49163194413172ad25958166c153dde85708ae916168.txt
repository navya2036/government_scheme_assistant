Welfare and Women Empowerment Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. The aim is to help poor widow mothers who face financial
hardship in conducting their daughter's marriage. The scheme provides financial assistance for the marriage of their
daughters.BenefitsFinancial assistance of â‚¹25,000/- given to the widow to meet the expenses incurred in the marriage of her daughter.1
