care.iii.There are considerable number of specialist doctors in urban private hospitals.iv. By entering into Public-Private partnership with
these hospitals, rural women can be provided with facilities for proper institutional deliveries.ï»¿The hospitals having requisite facilities
will be registered under the programme with the approval of District Health Society. The Hospitals will then sign an MOU with the
Department. Government Hospitals can also participate in this scheme.ï»¿Such registered hospitals will be paid Rs. 3.00 lakhs per 100

programme.The Government Hospitals will be paid Rs. 1.50 lakhs for every 100 deliveries, out of which 50,000 goes to the Health Care
Committee and the remaining is shared among the Hospital doctors, nurses and staff as per Yeshaswini guidelines.â€‹ï»¿ï»¿BenefitsThe

house.Registered private hospital will be available near her house.Candidate is not required to pay any charges right from the point of
admission to discharge.Free checkup and medicines will be provided.ï»¿EligibilityThe benefit is limited to the first two deliveries.The

forceps deliveries etc. These hospitals will be paid 10% ie., Rs.30,000 advance on participation in the scheme. This is to encourage more
and more private hospitals to participate in the program.The Government Hospitals will be paid Rs. 1.50 lakhs for every 100 deliveries,
