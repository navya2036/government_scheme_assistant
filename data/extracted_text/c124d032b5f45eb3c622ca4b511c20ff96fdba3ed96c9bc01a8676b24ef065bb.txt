DevelopmentStartupSubsidyDetailsThe key objective of the Gujarat Industrial policy 2020 is to encourage R&D and start innovation and
entrepreneurship. Hence, the new scheme is being introduced in order to support startups and innovation at different levels of the startup
cycle. In view of the strategy under Gujarat Industrial Policy, the Government is pleased to introduce a "Scheme for Assistance for
Startups / Innovation" which will come into force from 07/08/2020 and will remain in operation for a period of 5 years, i.e. upto
06.08.2025. ï»¿The scheme aims to give a further thrust to promoting startups and strengthening the ecosystem in the state, a task force
comprised of leading stakeholders in the Gujarat startup ecosystem. Gujarat, owing to its inherent strength of widespread entrepreneurial
spirit has gained a significant spot in the national startup ecosystem. The incentives to Startups under the Gujarat Industrial Policy 2015
have supported in the creation of a strong network of nodal institutions and several startups have expanded their operations/ products both
at the national and international levels.BenefitsAssistance to StartupsThe Quantum of Assistance as under would be provided to the
Startups whose innovative project is approved/sanctioned by SLEC. Sustenance Allowance:A sustenance allowance of â‚¹ 20,000 per
month per startup will be provided to the Startups for a period of one year.If startups have at least one woman co-founder, â‚¹ 25,000 per
month per startup will be provided as a sustenance allowance.ï»¿Seed Support:Seed support up to â‚¹ 30,00,000 will be provided to the
Startups on the following, based on approval/ sanction by SLEC:1. Cost of raw materials/consumables/hardware & software/professional
services /other equipment/ tools required for prototype/product/process development2. Market research Testing & Trials3. Marketing/
publicity assistance: Here marketing/ publicity assistance covers assistance to startups to attend national/international events. The
fees/charges paid for attending the same will be considered eligible under this head. [Note: However, it is clarified that to and fro
conveyance/accommodation expenses will not be considered for assistance]ï»¿Assistance to Startups having a Significant Impact on
Society:Additional support of upto â‚¹ 10,00,000 assistance will be provided to startups having innovative projects which have a
significant impact on society, based on the conditions on approval/ sanction given by the SLEC.ï»¿Pre-Series A funding:In the life cycle
of a Startup, there is a funding gap which is better identified as the valley of death. This is a phase post commercialization where the
Startup has just started generating revenue till the time period where its product/service gets market traction. Such startups will be
assisted in getting venture capital. Initially, to support, such mid-level Pre-series A funding to startups, a separate fund shall be created
under Gujarat Venture Finance Limited (GVFL). This assistance will be specifically to cater to the startups that have already received
seed support from the government under various schemes for the promotion of startups.ï»¿Interest subsidy:The startups that have been
approved/sanctioned under this scheme will be eligible to avail of an additional interest subsidy of 1%, a maximum of up to 9% on term
loans under the provisions of the scheme for assistance to MSMEs.ï»¿Assistance to enroll and participate in Acceleration
Program:Assistance up to â‚¹ 3,00,000 per startup will be provided to enroll and participate in the national / internationally recognized
acceleration program. Approved/sanctioned startups will be eligible for assistance up to â‚¹ 3,00,000 per startup during the operative
period of the scheme, to enroll/participate in a national/internationally recognized acceleration program. The fees/charges paid for
attending the same will be considered eligible for getting assistance. However, it is clarified that to and fro conveyance/accommodation
expenses will not be considered under this assistance.ï»¿Acceleration Program:An acceleration program is typically a 3-4 month program
for startups at the early traction and scaling stage. It is a process of intense, rapid, and immersive education aimed at accelerating the
growth of the startup.ï»¿Assistance for Skill Development:Approved/sanctioned startups shall be provided assistance for skill
development training specific to soft skills, managerial skills, marketing skills, and financing skills for the startup project. Such skill
development training should support the startups in understanding the ecosystem, fundraising, commercializing, and scaling up the
startup project. Financial assistance up to Rs. 1 lakh per approved/sanctioned startup shall be given for participating in such skill
development training programs on a reimbursement basis.ï»¿ï»¿Assistance to the Approved/Sanctioned Nodal Institution Mentoring
Assistance:Mentoring Assistance of â‚¹ 1,00,000 per approved/sanctioned startup shall be provided to Nodal institution for the mentoring
service (Maximum â‚¹ 15,00,000 per annum per institute.ï»¿Reimbursement Expenses on Organizing Promotional
Events:Reimbursement of 75% of expenses incurred for organizing promotional events to the extent of a maximum â‚¹ 5,00,000. If the
event being organized is specific to "Women Entrepreneurship", the Nodal Institution will be eligible for reimbursement of 90% of
expenses incurred for organizing promotional events to the extent of a maximum â‚¹ 5,00,000. However, it is clarified that the
beneficiary Nodal Institution will not be eligible to get assistance under any other scheme of Govt. of Gujarat for the same
component/purpose.ï»¿Other Support:1. Govt. will support approved/ sanctioned Startups to get free access to universities/ Libraries/
Govt. Laboratories/ SDCs (GIDC) / Center of Excellence / PSUs to support and facilitate innovative ideas/concepts.2. For organizing
Acceleration Programs in Gujarat any institution, would be assisted by providing financial assistance, as approved by SLEC, maximum
up to the matching contribution to the expenditure incurred.3. The project is entitled to avail of other benefits under the schemes for
assistance to MSMEs.ï»¿EligibilityFor Startups:Any individual/group of individuals having an innovative idea/concept and
recommended by an approved Nodal Institution will be eligible to get recognized as a startup under this scheme. The innovative project
can be from any sector or any faculty/branch of science/ technology/ engineering/ agriculture/ healthcare and no innovation worth its
name is restricted from availing assistance just because of its being in a particular sector, making this a sector agnostic scheme.ï»¿Note 1:
An entity formed by splitting up or reconstruction of an existing business shall not be considered a 'Startup'.Note 2: The startups which
are availing incentives as a 'startup' in any other 'startup scheme' of the Government of Gujarat will not get incentives under this scheme.
However, the startups/innovators availing benefits under the Student Startup and Innovation Policy (SSIP) would also be eligible to avail
of assistance under this scheme.ï»¿For Nodal Institution:Any University, Government approved/ affiliated/ recognized educational
institution, Incubation center, PSU, R&D Institutions, private and other establishments operating in Gujarat.The institution should be
registered under the Companies Act-2013, rule of Section 8, as a 'non-profit making company'.The institution should have separate
spaces and engage full-time employees for the purpose.ï»¿For Mentor:The mentor means a resource person who is registered under this
scheme and associated/recommended by the Nodal Institution.ExclusionsAn entity formed by splitting up or reconstruction of an existing
business shall not be considered a 'Startup'.The startups which are availing incentives as a 'startup' in any other 'startup scheme' of the
Government of Gujarat will not get incentives under this scheme. However, the startups/innovators availing benefits under the Student
Startup and Innovation Policy (SSIP) would also be eligible to avail of assistance under this scheme.Application ProcessOfflineFor
Approval/Sanction as Nodal Institutionï»¿Step 1: An eligible Institution intending to promote startups shall submit its proposal on the
online Portal to register itself as a Nodal Institution under this scheme.Step 2: The registered Nodal Institution shall apply to the IC office
with requisites in the prescribed format, for getting approval/sanction as a Nodal institution.Step 3: The applications will be scrutinized
by the Office of the Industries Commissioner and after due diligence, they will be placed before the SLEC for approval/ sanction.Step 4:
On approval from SLEC, the Office of Industries Commissioner will issue the Sanction letter to accord recognition as a Nodal

QuestionsWhat Is The Objective Of The Scheme For Assistance For Start-Ups/Innovation?Ans: The objective of the scheme is to
encourage and support start-ups and innovative projects in the state of Gujarat.Who Is Eligible To Apply For The Scheme?Ans: Any
individual, group of individuals, or registered companies in the state of Gujarat with innovative business ideas are eligible to apply for
the scheme.Is There Any Age Limit For The Applicant?Ans: No, there is no age limit for the applicant to apply for the scheme.What Is
The Maximum Amount Of Financial Assistance Provided Under The Scheme?Ans: The maximum amount of financial assistance
provided under the scheme is INR 50 lakhs.What Is The Duration Of The Scheme?Ans: The scheme is valid for a period of five years
from the date of implementation.What Is The Application Process For The Scheme?Ans: The application process for the scheme
involves filling an online application form and submitting the required documents.Can An Applicant Submit More Than One
Application?Ans: No, an applicant can submit only one application for the scheme.How Long Does It Take To Get A Response After
Submitting The Application?Ans: The response time for the application varies and may take up to 30 days.What Is The Selection
Process For The Scheme?Ans: The selection process for the scheme involves scrutiny of the application, site visits, and evaluation by
the selection committee.What Are The Evaluation Criteria For The Scheme?Ans: The evaluation criteria for the scheme include
innovation, market potential, social impact, sustainability, and financial viability of the project.Can An Applicant Modify The
Application After Submission?Ans: No, an applicant cannot modify the application after submission.What Are The Benefits Of The
Scheme?Ans: The benefits of the scheme include financial assistance, mentoring support, access to co-working spaces, and networking
opportunities.Is There Any Collateral Required For Availing The Financial Assistance?Ans: No, there is no collateral required for
availing the financial assistance.Is The Financial Assistance Provided As A Loan Or A Grant?Ans: The financial assistance is provided
as a grant and not as a loan.What Is The Repayment Period For The Financial Assistance?Ans: There is no repayment required for the
financial assistance provided under the scheme.Can A Beneficiary Of The Scheme Apply For Other Schemes Offered By The
Government?Ans: Yes, a beneficiary of the scheme can apply for other schemes offered by the government.Is There Any Deadline For
Submission Of The Application?Ans: No, there is no deadline for submission of the application.Can A Rejected Application Be Re-
Submitted?Ans: Yes, a rejected application can be re-submitted after incorporating the feedback provided by the selection committee.Is
The Scheme Applicable Only For New Start-Ups Or Can Existing Businesses Also Apply?Ans: Both new start-ups and existing
businesses with innovative ideas are eligible to apply for the scheme.Can A Non-Resident Indian (NRI) Apply For The Scheme?Ans:
No, NRIs are not eligible to apply for the scheme as it is meant only for residents of Gujarat.Sources And ReferencesGuidelinesStartup
