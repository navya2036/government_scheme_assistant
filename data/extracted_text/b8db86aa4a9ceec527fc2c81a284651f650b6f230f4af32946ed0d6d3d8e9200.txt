include Recognised Journalists.The Scheme also covers dependent family members of the journalist.Depending upon the nature and severity of the ailments/ disease, after taking into
consideration all relevant medical records, etc., the Committee may decide upon the fund to be made available per applicant in the following slabs: a) Up to a maximum of â‚¹ 50,000/-

the said accreditation/ recognition.ï»¿General Note: Grant of financial assistance to any working journalist is not a matter of right. Assistance would be extended depending on the
