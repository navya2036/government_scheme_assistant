Gargi Puraskar Yojana, girls are given incentives from the government to move forward in the field of education. Girls of all classes can
take advantage of the scheme. The amount of this scheme will be given every year on Basant Panchami.ï»¿The girl students who will get
the benefit of the scheme in class 10th will be required to enrol in the school in classes 11th and 12th. And the government will give the
amount to the beneficiary girl child through a check, so it is mandatory for the girl child to have a bank account. You can apply for the
scheme online.BenefitsPayable for 11 & 12 class girl students: Rs. 3,000/- each year on the day of Basant Panchami. EligibilityThe
student must have secured minimum 75% marks in class 10th.The girl student should be studying regularly in class 11 or 12.There is no
restriction on income, occupation and caste etc. of the parents of the student.Application ProcessOnlineScholarship Application online
through School Principal on Shaladarpan Portal.Click on "Balika Shiksha Protsahan" or click here. Select the scheme and click on apply
button. Now select the scheme name. Fill out the required details and process further. Documents RequiredEducation Qualification
Marksheet / Certificate Copy.Aadhar Card Copy.Bank Passbook Copy.Jan Aadhar/Bhamashah Card Copy.Frequently Asked
QuestionsWhat is the scope of this scheme?The benefit of Gargi Puraskar Yojana is given every year to the beneficiary girl child of
Rajasthan on the day of Basant Panchami. This time also the beneficiary girl students were taken on a tour of the girl students on the
occasion of Basant Panchami on January 29, 2020.What are the benefits under the scheme ?Payable for 11 & 12 class girl students: Rs.
3,000/- each year on Basant Panchami. Who can get the benefits of the scheme ?All Rajasthan State, students who full fill the eligibility
criteria. Is there caste restriction for students ?No, there is no such restriction. What Is The Education Qualification For The Scheme ?
The Girl student must have secured a minimum of 75% marks in class 10th and studying regularly in class 11 or 12.Is only gril student
get the scheme benefits ?Yes, Girl students will be eligible. What is the financial eligibility for applicant ?There is no restriction on
income.How To Apply For The Scheme ?1. Scholarship Application online through School Principal on Shaladarpan Portal. 2. Click on
"Balika Shiksha Protsahan" or click here. 3. Select the scheme and click on apply button. 4. Now select the scheme name. 5. Fill out the
required details and process further. What Documents Are Needed ?1. Education Qualification Marksheet / Certificate Copy. 2. Aadhar
Card Copy. 3. Bank Passbook Copy. 4. Jan Aadhar/Bhamashah Card Copy.What is the official web portal to apply ?Use the following
