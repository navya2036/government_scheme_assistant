exceeding three months during the tenure of the award on the recommendation of the supervisor/Head of the department of the
institution concerned. The period of leave without fellowship/associateship will be counted towards the tenure. EligibilityTarget Group:

conveys the provisional intimation of selection to the candidate. However, this intimation does not bestow the award to the candidate.
