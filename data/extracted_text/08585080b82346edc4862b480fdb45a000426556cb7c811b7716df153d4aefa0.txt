Chhattisgarh State Government started a scheme "Kisan Samriddhi Yojana" for farmer.What are the benefits?Financial Assistance.Who
can get the benefits?1. Farmers of all categories of districts will be benefited. 2. Preference will be given to small and marginal
farmers.Can people from other states also apply for this scheme?No. What is the financial eligibility for applicant ?There is no financial
eligibility.What is the minimum age eligibility ?The age of the applicant should be above 18 years.How To Apply For The Scheme ?1.
Applicant farmer need to approach panchayat first. 2. The selection of farmers will be done with the approval of the three-tier
panchayat.What are the required documents?1. Aadhaar Card. 2. Land paper. 3. Photograph. 4. Bill Invoice.Sources And
