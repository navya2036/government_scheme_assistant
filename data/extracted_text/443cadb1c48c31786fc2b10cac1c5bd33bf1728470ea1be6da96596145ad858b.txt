SamgamDetailsObjectivesThe main objective of this scheme is to provide financial assistance in the form of unemployment allowance to
the educated youth of the state who are searching for employment. This scheme helps those who are educated and talented but cannot
generate enough income to apply for government jobs or other jobs due to the financial crisis. BenefitsBenefitsThe government will
provide financial assistance of Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 1,500 till the time youths get desired job.Private and government jobs are available on
one portal.Online application facility.Facility to search jobs by category, location, department, and salary.EligibilityEligibilityThe
candidate must be a permanent resident of Uttar Pradesh.Candidates must have passed at-least 10th standard (High School)
examinations.The candidate must be currently unemployed i.e he must not be not working in any private or government job.Moreover, all
the applicants must lie in the age group between 21-35 years.The total family income of the candidates from all sources must not exceed
Rs. 3 lahks per annum.Application ProcessOnlineApplication ProcessFirstly visit the official website.ï»¿On the homepage, click the

(mark sheets, certificates, etc.).Frequently Asked QuestionsWhat is UP Berojgari Bhatta Yojna ?This scheme helps those who are
educated and talented but cannot generate enough income to apply for government jobs or other jobs due to the financial crisis. What are
the benefits ?1. The government will provide financial assistance of Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 1,500 till the time youths get their desired jobs. 2.
Private and government jobs are available on one portal. 3. Online application facility. 4 .Facility to search jobs by category, location,
department, and salary.What is the Eligibility ?1. The candidate must be a permanent resident of Uttar Pradesh. 2. Candidates must have
passed at-least 10th standard (High School) examinations. 3. The candidate must be currently unemployed i.e he must not be not working
in any private or government job. 4. Moreover, all the applicants must lie in the age group between 21-35 years. 5. The total family
