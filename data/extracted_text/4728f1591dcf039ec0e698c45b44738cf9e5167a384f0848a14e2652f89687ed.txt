Shortlisted Candidates - ï»¿70 % weightage will be given for the medals won in various International championships and sports events of
the disciplines covered in Olympic Games (Summer, Winter and Paralympics), Asian Games and Commonwealth Games. ï»¿Points for
winning medals will be given to each eligible sportsperson for his/her performance during his entire sporting career as per marks given in
the table below:ï»¿1. Olympic Games/ Paralympics: Gold: 80, Silver: 70, Bronze: 55.2. World Championship/World Cup (once in 4
years )* : Gold: 40, Silver: 30, Bronze: 20.3. Asian Games: Gold: 30, Silver: 25, Bronze: 20.4. Commonwealth Games: Gold: 25, Silver:
20, Bronze: 15.ï»¿1. For World Championship/World Cup of different periodicities, proportionate marks will be given.2. Equivalence of
any other sports discipline/tournament, not listed above, will be decided by the Selection Committee with reference to above-mentioned
categories of sports tournaments and the points will be allotted accordingly.3. For team events, marks will be computed taking into
consideration strength of the team. If prescribed marks for an individual event are 'A', in the case of team event, the marks will be
computed as follows:(i) A team of 2 persons: 1.5 X `A'/number of persons in the team(ii) A team of 3 or 4 persons: 2 X 'A'/number of
persons in the team(iii) A team of 5 to 10 persons: 3 X `A'/number of persons in the team(iv) A team of more than 10 persons: 5 X
`A'/number of persons in the team4. 70 marks will be given to the sportsperson getting maximum points as computed in the table above.
Rest of the sportspersons will be given marks proportionately to the said sportsperson. For example, if a sportsperson 'A' has got highest
points say 45 points as per the table above and another sportsperson 'B' has got 40 points as per the table above, then 'A' will get 70 marks
and 'B' will get ( 70 x 40 /45) = 62.22 marks.5. 30 % weightage will be given to the marks given by â€¢ the Selection Committee for
assessment of the eligible sportsperson for contribution made for promotion of sports after retiement from active sports career e.g. as a
coach, sports manager, etc. and for qualities of sportsmanship, leadership and sense of discipline.6. For cricket and indigenous games, not
included in Olympic / Asian Games / Commonwealth Games disciplines, the Selection Committee will take into consideration their
individual performances and contribution to sports after active sports career.7. Not more than one award will be given in each discipline
in a particular year. This condition would be relaxed only in exceptional circumstances to be recorded in writing.8. Not more than 3
awards could be given in any calendar year. This condition will be released only in exceptional circumstances to be recorded in writing.9.
In a particular sports discipline, names of not more than 2 sportspersons (1 male and 1 female) with the highest marks as per the criteria
brought out in the table above will be considered by the Selection Committee,10. Sometimes, the Selection Committee may feel that as
per the given criteria, a very deserving sportsperson in a team game may not get Dhyan Chand Award. In such a situation, it may
recommend such name giving full justification in writing.11. Selection Committee will recommend the final list of sportspersons for
Dhyan Chand Award as per the marks obtained in terms of the criteria indicated above. 12. Selection Committee may not mechanically
recommend Dhyan Chand Award to the recipient of the highest marks across disciplines. But in a particular sports discipline, award will
