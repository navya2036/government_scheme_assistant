BILLIARDS & SNOOKER : The cash award in Billiards and Snooker will be given to the medal winners and their coaches on winning
medals in IBSF World Billiards Championship (150 up and Long up format), ISBF World Snooker Championship (both World
championships held annually) and ACBS Asian Billiards Championship, ACBS Asian Snooker Championship (both Asian
Championships held annually) events held annually. The amount of award money will be calculated as per para A .If in an international
sports event, more than one medal is won by a sportsperson (in individual or team category), he/she will be eligible for cash award only
for the highest medal won by him/her in that event. B. Incentive to Indian Coaches:1. The amount of the cash award payable to the
coaches will be as under : In case of Individual events, award money to be given to the set of coaches will be 50% of the award money
payable to the sportsperson (s) whom the set of coaches had coached.In case of team events, award money given to the set of coaches of
the medal winning team will be the same as to the set of coaches as stated above for similar medal. The set of coaches could comprise of
1 or more coaches. In case, the set contains more than one coach, the said award money will be distributed among all the coaches in the

set, equally. 2. The amount of cash award payable to coaches of medal winners in individual events will be distributed among the
following types of coaches in the ratio mentioned against each category : Coach at grass root level who initially spotted the talent and
groomed: 30% of the amount payable to the coaches.Coach at the developmental level who further groomed and nurtured the medal
winning sportspersons which would also include personal coach : 30% of the amount payable to the coaches.Coach at the elite level who
have been part of the coaching camps or imparted training to the medal winner for at least 180 days in the preceding two year period
before the event : 40% of the amount payable to coach(es).3. Names for coach at grassroot level and coach at the developmental level
will be as recommended by the medal winning sportsperson. However, spouse, father, mother, brother and sister of the sportspersons
will not be eligible for cash award in these cases.4. For the Coach(es) at the elite level who have been part of the coaching camps or
imparted training to the medal winner for at least 180 days in the preceding two year period before the event, the set of coaches could
comprise of 1 or more coaches. In case, the set contains more than one coach, the said award money will be distributed among all the
coaches in the set, equally.5. The Cash Awards for the Coach(es) at the elite level will be given regardless of whether a coach is a Chief
coach or Assistant Coach subject to the condition that the coach should have been associated with the training of the athlete/team on a
regular basic for at least a minimum period of 180 days, i.e., each coach in the set of coaches should have been associated with the medal
winning sportsperson / team for at least 180 days on a regular basis. 6. Sports Authority of India (SAI) will devise a mechanism and build
up a database about the grassroot coaches, developmental coaches including personal coaches for verifying the recommendations made
by the medal winning sportsperson for giving cash award.7. If in an event, more than one medal is won by different individual
sportspersons trained by the same coach, the coach will get the cash award only for the highest medal. 8. If there is any doubt/dispute
about the names of coaches recommended for cash award, the Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports will have the final say in the matter
after due deliberations.EligibilityEligible Sports DisciplinesThe Awards will be given in the following disciplines :Sports disciplines in

The Objective of the Scheme is to incentivize the achievements of outstanding sportspersons, encourage and motivate them for higher
achievements and to act as inspiring role models for attracting the younger generation to take up sports.What Are The Sporting
Disciplines In Which The Award If Conferred?The Awards will be given in the following disciplines : (i) Sports disciplines in Olympic

may be increased once in four years, by the Minister-in-charge of Youth Affairs & Sports on the recommendations of a Committee
chaired by the Secretary (Sports), which will, inter alia, include the Financial Adviser of the Ministry.What If In An Event, More Than
One Medal Is Won By Different Individual Sportspersons Trained By The Same Coach?If in an event, more than one medal is won by
different individual sportspersons trained by the same coach, the coach will get the cash award only for the highest medal. What If There
