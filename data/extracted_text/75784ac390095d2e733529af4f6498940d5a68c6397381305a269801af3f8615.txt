from another state but working as a construction worker in Delhi, can | get registered?Yes, a Construction Worker native to any place in
India, but working in Delhi can register with the DBoCWWB. The Construction Workers must own an Aadhar Card from any place in

workers must be at least 18 years old but not more than 60 years old to be eligible for registration under Section 12 of the Main Act.
