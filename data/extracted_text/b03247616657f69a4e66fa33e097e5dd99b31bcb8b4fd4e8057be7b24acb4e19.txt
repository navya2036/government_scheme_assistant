CBOCWWB?The "Scholarship to the Ward of Beneficiary" is a scheme initiated by the Chandigarh Building and Other Construction
Workers Welfare Board (CBOCWWB), Labour Department, Chandigarh. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance in the form of
scholarships to the children or wards of workers who are registered under the CBOCWWB.Who is eligible to apply for this scholarship?
The scholarship is available for the children/wards of workers who are registered under the CBOCWWB.What are the criteria for
awarding scholarships under this scheme?Scholarships are granted based on the percentage of marks obtained by the eligible candidates
in their 10th, 12th, and Graduation examinations.How is the scholarship amount determined?The scholarship amount is determined based
on the percentage of marks obtained by the applicant in their 10th, 12th, and Graduation examinations.What are the eligibility criteria for
workers/beneficiaries to avail benefits under the scholarship scheme?To be eligible, the worker/beneficiary must be employed in an
establishment registered under the Punjab Labour Welfare Fund Act, 1965. Additionally, their contributions towards the labour welfare
fund should be regularly received by the Board.When can a worker/beneficiary start availing benefits under the scheme?A
worker/beneficiary can avail benefits of the scheme only after being a member of the Chandigarh Building and Other Construction
Workers Welfare Board for at least six months.Are there any restrictions on scholarship applications based on academic performance?
Yes, scholarship applications will not be entertained if the student has received a compartment in any subject. Can a worker's child
receive a scholarship from both the Board and another institution simultaneously?No, if a scholarship is granted to the child of a worker
from any other institution, they will not be entitled to receive a scholarship from the Board.Are there special provisions for students
excelling in sports?Yes, students securing 1st, 2nd, or 3rd place in National/International games/sports will be granted a scholarship
without considering the percentage of marks.How many children of a worker can receive the scholarship?A worker can avail the
