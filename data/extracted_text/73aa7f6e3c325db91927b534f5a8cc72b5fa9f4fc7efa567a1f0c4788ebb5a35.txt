Mukhyamantri Vridhjan Pension Yojna is an old age pension scheme initiated by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of
Bihar. It aims to provide financial assistance to elderly citizens aged 60 years and above.Who is eligible for the pension under this
scheme?Individuals who are 60 years or above are eligible for the pension under the scheme.What are the benefits offered under this
scheme?1. For individuals aged 60-79 years, a monthly pension of â‚¹400/- will be provided. 2. Individuals aged 80 years and above will

disbursed on a monthly basis.Can retired government employees apply for this pension scheme?No, individuals who are retired
government employees are not eligible to avail the benefits of the scheme.Is there any restriction on receiving pensions from other
sources?Yes, applicants should not be receiving any pension from government service, family pension, or social security pension from
