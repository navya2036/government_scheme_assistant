children per family are eligible to avail of the benefits. The benefits are provided for a period of maximum of 4 years for first-year
admitted students and a maximum of 3 years for second-year admitted students through lateral entry. This scheme is an attempt to give
young women the opportunity to further their education and prepare for a successful future by empowering Women through knowledge,
