CancelApply NowCheck EligibilityHimachal PradeshPartial Travel Support SchemePartialScholarScientistTravelDetailsThe Himachal
Pradesh Council for Science, Technology & Environment (HIMCOSTE), has a scheme for providing partial travel assistance to
deserving bright young Scientists and Research Scholars working in an academic or research institution located in Himachal Pradesh for
participating in a National/ and International Conference/ Seminar/ Symposium/ Workshop held within India or abroad. The scheme
does not provide assistance towards lodging, boarding, maintenance, registration fees, taxi fare and other costsï»¿BenefitsPartial
Coverage: The scheme provides support for a portion of the total travel expenses incurred by the beneficiaries.Financial Assistance: For
international travel, the scheme offers support up to a maximum of Rs. 60,000/- or 60% of the total travel expenses, whichever is lower.
This financial assistance helps offset a significant portion of the travel costs for international trips.In-Country Travel Support: For travel
within the country, the scheme provides a maximum support of Rs. 10,000/- or 60% of the total travel expenses, whichever is lower. This
assistance helps beneficiaries cover a portion of their travel costs within the country.Flexibility: The scheme allows beneficiaries to
utilize the financial support as per their travel requirements, be it international or in-country travel.State Council Support: The State
Council is responsible for providing the specified travel support under this scheme, ensuring that beneficiaries receive the designated
financial assistance.EligibilityEducational Qualification: The applicant should have passed at least M.Sc./B.E./B.Tech/MBBS or an
equivalent degree from a recognized University/Institute.Age Limit: The applicant's age should not exceed 40 years on the date of
commencement of the Conference/Seminar/Symposium/Workshop.Paper Acceptance: The applicant's paper should have been accepted
for presentation. The application must include a copy of the full paper, an abstract, the schedule of the conference, and an acceptance
letter from the organizer.Previous Travel Support: Scientists and Research Scholars who have not received travel support under PATSS
in the previous three years are eligible for consideration.International Reputation: The Conference/Seminar/Symposium/Workshop
should have an international character and repute. Applications to attend annual meetings of professional societies will not be
considered.Publication Requirement: Scientists and Research Scholars must have published at least one paper in a refereed journal to be
eligible for consideration.Forwarding of Application: The application, along with all necessary documents, should be forwarded through
the proper channel from the Head of the Institution.Application Deadline: Complete applications should reach the office one month
before the event takes place.Research Paper Reprint: The Scientist/Research scholar sanctioned travel grant under the scheme is required
to provide one reprint of the research paper presented at the Conference/Seminar/Symposium/Workshop after its publication.Notification
of Selection: The applicants recommended by the Committee/Experts will be informed of their selection via email or post, using the
email ID provided by the candidate.ExclusionsLodging and Boarding: The scheme does not provide assistance for expenses related to
lodging and boarding during the conference, seminar, symposium, workshop, or any other event.Maintenance: The scheme does not
cover maintenance expenses, which include costs for meals, daily allowances, or any other personal expenses incurred during the travel
period.Registration Fees: The scheme does not provide financial assistance for registration fees associated with the conference, seminar,
symposium, workshop, or any other event. The participants are responsible for covering these fees themselves.Taxi Fare: The scheme
excludes assistance for taxi fares or any other transportation costs incurred during the travel, including local transportation at the
destination or airport transfers.Other Costs: The scheme does not cover miscellaneous costs that may arise during the travel, such as visa
fees, travel insurance, printing of conference materials, or any other expenses not directly related to the travel itself.Application
ProcessOfflineObtain and Fill Application Form: Obtain the application form for the PATSS scheme. Complete the application form and
provide your bio-data as required.Attach Essential Documents: Ensure that the following essential documents are properly attached and
tagged with the application:One copy of the completed application form and bio-data.Degree certificate (proof of eligibility).Date of
birth certificate.Copy of the letter from another national or international agency committing to bear partial travel costs.Detailed letter of
invitation from the conference organizers.Abstract of the paper you will be presenting at the conference.Certificate from the concerned
airline indicating the cost of return airfare by economy class via the shortest route3. Check for Completeness: Double-check that all the
necessary documents mentioned above are properly attached to the application. Incomplete applications will not be entertained.4. Submit
the Application: Send the completed application, along with the required enclosures, to the Himachal Pradesh Council for Science,
Technology & Environment (HIMCOSTE) at least one month prior to the commencement of the conference. The application must be
forwarded by the Head of the Institution.5. Selection Process: The HIMCOSTE will review the applications and communicate the
decision about one week before the conference's commencement. No interim correspondence will be entertained.6. Commitment Letter
and Reimbursement: If selected, the HIMCOSTE will issue a commitment letter agreeing to provide the support. The committed amount
will be reimbursed to the scientist upon their return to India after participating in the conference. The scientist must submit a detailed
report, a copy of the air ticket, etc., within two months from the conference's date.Documents RequiredOne copy of completed
application form and bio-data. Degree certificate (Eligibility) Date of birth certificate. A copy of letter from other national or
international agency committing to bear partial cost of travel. Detailed letter of Invitation from organizers. Abstract of the paper to be
presented by the applicant at the conference. A certificate from concerned airlines indicating cost of return air fare by economy class by
shortest route. Frequently Asked QuestionsWho is eligible to apply for the PATSS scheme?Scientists and Research Scholars working in
an academic or research institution located in Himachal Pradesh, who have passed at least M.Sc./B.E./B.Tech/MBBS or equivalent
degree from a recognized University/Institute, are eligible to apply.What is the maximum age limit to be considered for travel assistance?
The applicant's age should not be above 40 years on the date of commencement of the Conference/Seminar/Symposium/Workshop.Is it
mandatory for the paper to be accepted for presentation?Yes, the applicant's paper should have been accepted for presentation. A copy of
the full paper, along with an abstract, schedule of the conference, and acceptance letter from the organizer, must be appended with the
application.Can scientists and research scholars who have previously received travel support under PATSS apply again?No, scientists
and research scholars who have received travel support under PATSS in the previous three years are not eligible for consideration.Are
applications to attend annual meetings of professional societies considered under this scheme?No, applications to attend annual meetings
of professional societies will not be considered. The Conference/Seminar/Symposium/Workshop should be of an international character
and repute.Is publication in a refereed journal necessary to be considered for travel assistance?Yes, scientists and research scholars who
have not published at least one paper in a refereed journal would not be considered.What is the procedure for forwarding the application?
The application, along with all necessary documents, should be forwarded through the proper channel from the Head of the
Institution.What is the deadline for submitting the application?Applications complete in all respects should reach the office mandatorily
one month before the event.What is the requirement for the research paper presented at the conference?The scientist/research scholar
sanctioned travel grant under the scheme is required to provide one reprint of the research paper presented at the conference after its
