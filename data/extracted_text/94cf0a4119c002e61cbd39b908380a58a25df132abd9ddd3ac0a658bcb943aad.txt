application.Frequently Asked QuestionsWhat are the National Water Awards?The National Water Awards are an initiative to recognize
and celebrate outstanding achievements and contributions in the field of water resources management, conservation, and sustainability
across India.Who organizes the National Water Awards?The awards are typically organized by the Ministry of Jal Shakti, Government
of India, or other relevant government agencies responsible for water resources management.What types of achievements do the National
Water Awards recognize?The awards recognize achievements in various areas such as water conservation, rainwater harvesting,
groundwater recharge, efficient water use, wastewater treatment and reuse, river rejuvenation, and community-based water
management.Who is eligible to participate in Awards?Individuals, organizations, institutions, local bodies, NGOs, industries, and
government agencies involved in water conservation and management activities in India are eligible to participate.Can projects/initiatives
from all states participate?Yes, projects/initiatives from all states and union territories of India are eligible to participate in the National
Water Awards.Is there a fee to participate in the National Water Awards?No, there is usually no participation fee required to enter the
